# 📚 PSCS_46 — Application for Assessment of Quality of Textbook / Reference Book / E-Book

## 📖 Overview
Medical students in ASU (Ayurveda, Unani, Siddha) often face challenges in identifying quality textbooks. Many books claim **NCISM** compliance but vary widely in accuracy and credibility. Substandard resources mislead students and negatively impact learning outcomes.

The **National Commission for Indian System of Medicine (NCISM)** has developed an official *Assessment Scale* to evaluate book quality. However, the current **manual review process** is slow, cumbersome, and lacks nationwide reach.

**PSCS_46** is a **web-based application** designed to streamline the assessment process, enabling faster, more reliable evaluations of textbooks, reference books, and e-books across India.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
- **Current Issue:** Manual assessment is slow, labor-intensive, and prone to inconsistencies.
- **Impact:** Students and educators cannot easily identify the most credible learning resources.
- **Goal:** Automate and digitize the assessment process to improve efficiency, accessibility, and transparency.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- **NCISM-Compliant Evaluation** — Built-in scoring system aligned with official guidelines.
- **Nationwide Participation** — Allow experts, reviewers, and institutions to submit and review assessments online.
- **User-Friendly Interface** — Designed for ease of use by academicians and administrators.
- **Automated Reporting** — Instant generation of quality reports and recommendations.
- **Search & Filter** — Quickly locate high-quality books by category, author, or score.
- **Data Security** — Secure authentication for reviewers and administrators.
