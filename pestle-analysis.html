Key Risk Mitigation Priorities:

  1. Legal Compliance: Establish comprehensive legal framework and regular compliance audits
  2. Economic Stability: Implement cost-effective architecture and secure diverse funding
  3. Technology Resilience: Build modular, scalable system with multiple fallback options
  4. Political Adaptability: Monitor regulatory changes and maintain flexible system design
  5. Social Acceptance: Focus on user experience and comprehensive training programs
  6. Environmental Responsibility: Adopt sustainable computing practices and green technologies
